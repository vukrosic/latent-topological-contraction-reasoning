Topological Contraction Reasoning (TCR)

Modern Large Language Models (LLMs) process information through a fixed sequence of layers.
This limits their "thinking time" to a constant value regardless of problem complexity.
TCR treats reasoning as a dynamic process in latent space rather than a static feed-forward pass.
We model the reasoning step as a Contraction Mapping.
This provides a mathematical guarantee that a recursive latent state will converge to a unique, stable "thought" — a fixed point.
Convergence properties are derived mathematically using the Banach Fixed Point Theorem.
The work includes a code implementation and a small-scale Proof-of-Concept demonstrating the stability of the recursive training loop.
This serves as a conceptual and technical foundation for future research into variable-depth reasoning architectures.
